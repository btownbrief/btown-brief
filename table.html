HOW THE TABLE WORKS
  • PROP THE PHONE UP — cards rotate on a slow clock; the screen stays awake
  • TAP THE CARD to hold it while someone answers · tap again to release
  • ⏮ ⏭ step back to a card you missed, or deal the next one
  • PACE — RELAXED for deep tables, LIVELY for a speed round
  • DECKS — strike out anything you don't want dealt
  • SYNC — every phone with it on shows the same card at the same time (no pairing, it's the clock) · pace and decks pin while synced · a tap-hold pauses just this phone and rejoins on release
  • + up top gets another phone or the iPad on this board

THE PAGE ASKS THE PHONE TO STAY AWAKE, BUT THE OS HAS THE LAST WORD — IF THE SCREEN STILL DIMS, BUMP AUTO-LOCK IN SETTINGS FOR THE HOUR.
