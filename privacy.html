Privacy

What this site collects, in plain words

The Btown Brief is one person in Burlington, Vermont. This page covers guide.btownbrief.com, play.btownbrief.com and hub.btownbrief.com, and the forms on them. Last updated 2026-09-06.

The newsletter

Subscribing gives your email address to Beehiiv, the company that sends the newsletter. Beehiiv records opens and link clicks so we can tell sponsors how many people read an edition; we never share who. You can unsubscribe from any edition in one click, and the address goes with it. Beehiiv's own policy is at beehiiv.com/privacy.

Forms on this site

The partner form asks for your name, business, role, what you sell, a budget range, and how to reach you. Those answers are used to reply to you and for nothing else. You are not added to any mailing list by filling in a form. Until a form service is connected the form opens your own mail app, so the details travel as an ordinary email.

Other pages offer a mailto link for tips, corrections and photos. Those are plain emails to us.

Analytics and pixels

GoatCounter counts page views without cookies and without identifying you; the counts are aggregate. The Meta pixel is present on the guide pages so that if we ever run an ad on Facebook or Instagram we can tell whether it brought anyone here. Meta may set a cookie for that; you can block it with your browser's tracking protection or an ad blocker and the site works the same. Google Fonts serves the typefaces and sees your browser's request for them.

The games and apps

Leaderboards, rooms and "I'm in" lists store what you type (usually a first name, a score, or an email where a page says so) with Supabase, the database behind the fleet. A random token in your browser's storage is the only identity; there are no accounts and no passwords. What is shown publicly is first names and counts, never emails. Clearing site data forgets the token.

What we never do

Sell or rent a list. Share an individual reader's address, opens or clicks with a sponsor. Buy data about you. Run ads that follow you around the internet.

Ask, fix, remove

Email steve@btownbrief.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it removed. A person answers, usually within a day.
