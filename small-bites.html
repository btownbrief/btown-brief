A quick heads-up on these menus

Everything here was gathered from what each place publishes online — its own site, a menu PDF, a listing. None of it comes from the restaurants directly.

Plenty of kitchens don't keep their own menus current. Prices drift, dishes get pulled, whole menus go stale — and there's no way for us to know from the outside.

So take this as a rough idea of what's around and what it costs, not a promise. Each place shows the date its menu was last fetched. If a specific dish or price is the reason you're walking over, call first.
