    You can be on this page by Sunday

    Photograph the weed growing out of a crack on Pine Street and it counts. That is the whole barrier to entry. Both apps are free.

    Plants, bugs, mushrooms, mammals

    1. Get iNaturalist (iPhone, Android) and make an account.
    2. Photograph anything wild inside Burlington. The app suggests what it is; the community confirms.
    3. That is it. Anything observed inside the city limits lands in Biodiversity of Burlington, Vermont automatically, and in these counts within six hours.
    4. Want it to count for the state too? Join the Vermont Atlas of Life project.

    Birds

    1. Get eBird (free, from the Cornell Lab) and make an account.
    2. Start a checklist at a Burlington hotspot: the hotspot map for the city, or just where you are.
    3. Submit it. Your name goes on eBird's own Chittenden County top 100 and, once the feed here is on, this page.

    Next big one: the City Nature Challenge, April 30 to May 3, 2027. We would like Burlington to show up properly.
