Why an email beats an impression

Most local advertising is bought by the thousand impressions, which means you pay for people who never saw it. An email is different. Someone opened it, on purpose, on a Monday morning, because they wanted to know what was happening in their town this week.

About 6 in 10 people on this list open every single issue. The list is Burlington-area, and the newsletter is about Burlington and nowhere else. Where exactly each reader lives is what the sign-up survey is collecting now.
