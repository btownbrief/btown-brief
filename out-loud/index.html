    How it works

    Tap Start walking and keep the phone in your hand or front pocket with the screen on — like walking directions. Your phone stops reporting location when the screen is off, so use Pocket mode to dim it instead of locking it. Each story plays once; the next one waits until you reach it.

    Eyes up at crossings. One earbud is plenty.

    The voice and the facts

    Every story is written by a person from cited sources and reviewed before it goes live — tap Where this comes from under any story. The narration is a synthetic voice; until the studio recordings land, your phone's built-in voice reads the script.

    Free, no account, nothing tracked beyond a count of how many times each story plays. Your "heard" marks live only on this phone.

    Offline

    Stories keep playing without signal once you save them. The map needs a connection.
