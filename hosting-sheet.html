Running the table this week

You're not in charge of anything. You're the person who got there first and knows where the table is. That's the whole job.

Setup, five lines

  1. 9:50. Zero Gravity, 716 Pine. Take the big table on the right. If it's taken, take the next biggest and stand the card where the door can see it.
  2. Card out. Prop the "Come sit" table card facing the door. First-timers find the card, not you.
  3. Phone up. Open guide.btownbrief.com/table.html?sync=1 on your phone, lean it against a mug or the card so the table can read it. It rotates on its own. Anyone else can scan the card's QR and their phone shows the same thing.
  4. Stickers and the tally. The "I came alone" stickers and the paper tally live in the bag with the card. One line per name per Saturday. It's how people earn the shirts.
  5. Leave when you like. Hand the card to whoever is staying. It keeps the table open after you.

When someone new walks up

"Yes, this is the Btown table, grab a chair. What part of town are you coming from?"

Two sentences. Then introduce them to one person by name and let go. Don't run an icebreaker; the phone on the table is the icebreaker if the room goes quiet.

If someone says they nearly didn't come in, give them the sticker.

What the card promises

  • Free; buy your own coffee
  • No agenda
  • Coming alone is normal
  • Twenty minutes counts
  • 10 to noon; people drift in and out

Keep those true and nothing else matters.

Afterward, two minutes

  • Photo of the table (ask first, backs of heads are fine) → Photo Wall in the reader community, or Telegram.
  • Anything someone asked for ("does anyone know a plumber", "two more for basketball") → post it in Asks & offers (community, General channel) with their first name if they said okay.
  • Head count and any first-timers' names → Telegram to Steve, so the second-visit note goes out.

Something off? Text Steve. Anything you're unsure about, the answer is "sit down and have a coffee."
