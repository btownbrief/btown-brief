Insertions: the Brief publishes Monday and Friday, roughly nine editions a month. A month of Anchor or Featured is nine insertions; Starter is four. Price for this proposal:   per month, set on the call by category.

The terms

Creative. We make your ad. You give us the offer, your logo and a photo, or we shoot one. We write and design it to match your brand and our template; you approve it before it runs; revisions included. No charge for design, and we do not accept finished ads from outside.

Reporting. A one-page report on the third of every month: editions your ad ran in, total opens across them, unique clicks on your ad, how it compares to last month, which creative did best, and one suggestion.

Makegood. If an edition carrying your ad fails to send, or your ad is omitted, or a link in it is broken, we add an equivalent insertion at no charge in the next available edition. If we cannot, that insertion is credited. You do not have to ask.

Term and cancellation. Anchor and Featured are three months minimum; Category Exclusive is twelve; Starter, Gift Guide and one-offs have no term. Month to month after the minimum with 30 days' written notice. Inside the minimum you may cancel for any reason and we run out the insertions already paid for. We may also end a sponsorship with 30 days' notice and a prorated refund if the fit stops working.

Category protection. Anchor and Featured sponsors get first refusal on their category: before we sell to a direct competitor we tell you, and you have five business days to take the slot. Category Exclusive is absolute for the term across the newsletter, Instagram, the guide and our events. Categories are defined in writing when you sign.

Payment. Prepaid monthly by card or invoice. Annual exclusives quarterly or annual in advance. First insertion runs after the first payment clears.

What we won't do

More than two sponsors in an edition. National brands. Ads that pretend to be editorial. Anything we would not recommend to a friend.
