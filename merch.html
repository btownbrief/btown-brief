Know your enemy

Zebra Mussel

Dreissena polymorpha. Drawn like a prized specimen in a Victorian field guide.

The zebra mussel is the undeniable villain of the lake, so it gets the know-your-enemy treatment: a sharp, invasive nuisance turned into a surprisingly intricate, vintage-looking biology specimen. Fine-line ink on a blue jean Comfort Colors tee.

For the person who has cut a foot on one and still went back in.

Champus Fossil

Champus tanystropheus. Not a mascot. Evidence.

This strips away the cartoon entirely and treats Champ like a prehistoric biology specimen. It reads less like a souvenir and more like a page torn from an old field journal, which is how the true believers prefer it.

For anyone who has squinted at the lake at dusk and seen something.

Class of whenever

University of the Pit

UNIVERSITY OF THE PIT, over a tower crane. Go team.

A classic collegiate shirt for Burlington's most famous hole in the ground. It commemorates the decade we all spent waiting for something to rise from the ashes of the old Church Street mall. If you walked past it every day, you earned the degree.

For everyone who gave directions using the Pit as a landmark.

Local lore

World's Tallest Filing Cabinet

A wireframe, and three lines of specs: 38 ft · flynn_ave · arts_district.

The world's tallest filing cabinet is a piece of local lore that defies explanation, so it finally gets the abstract technical drawing it deserves. The real one has a few more drawers. This one hits the right notes.

For the person who brings out-of-town visitors to Flynn Avenue on purpose.

State religion

Grade A Twist

GRADE A TWIST, over a creemee that means it.

The maple creemee is basically our state religion, but most shirts treat it like a joke. This one gives it the reverence of a vintage culinary brand, the kind of label you would find on a tin in your grandmother's pantry.

For creemee people. You know if you are one.

The honest months

Burlington Gray

BURLINGTON GRAY, under bare branches. The colour of the view out your window from November to April.

Tourists chase the green summers and the orange autumns. This design honors the honest, slate-gray reality of the months in between, matched to mud season and the long wait for the lake to thaw. Also available embroidered, for people who commit.

For people who stay through March and would not live anywhere else.

The mascot

The Lab

BTOWN BRIEF, around a black Lab in reading glasses with the paper.

The newsletter's mascot, drawn from Roxy and Phoebe. She feels trustworthy, and like she has been delivering the local news since 1955 with those studious glasses on. This is the one people stop you about.

For dog people, subscribers, and anyone who has met her on the bike path.

New · Saturday coffee

Burlington Small Talk Department

Front: BURLINGTON SMALL TALK DEPARTMENT. Back: “So what brought you to Vermont?” Est. every Saturday.

Saturday coffee is, if we are honest, a small talk department. This is its uniform, and the back carries the opening question every table asks at least once. Cream Comfort Colors tee, brown ink, front and back.

For coffee regulars, and anyone who has answered that question forty times and still enjoys it.

New · a gentle defense

The lake is doing its best

The lake is doing its best. A tee, and a three-inch sticker for the water bottle.

Champlain on a gray day, which is most days, deserves someone in its corner. This is that person. Lora italics on cream, and the sticker version costs about what a creemee does.

New · the cap

Pedestrian

PEDESTRIAN, embroidered small, with CHURCH ST. MARKETPLACE underneath.

Church Street is a pedestrian marketplace, which makes this the only title the street will ever give you. Embroidered on a cream dad cap in brown thread. Wear it to the market, wear it to the Pit, wear it while jaywalking on Main and reflect on the irony.

For hat people, and people who walk everywhere and want that on record.
