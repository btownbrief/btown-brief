Burlington reads this newsletter twice a week. About 3,100 people get it, and about 6 in 10 open it every time.

That second number is the one that matters. Most email you send to a list of three thousand people is read by a few hundred of them. This one is read by nearly 2,000, on a Monday morning and a Friday morning, by a Burlington-area audience. The list spans Burlington, South Burlington, Winooski, Essex, Shelburne, Williston and Colchester.

Two sponsors an edition. That's the limit.

Not twenty. Two. Your business does not scroll past in a column of other businesses, because there is no column.

What you get

  • We make the ad. You tell us what you want people to do. We write it and design it to match your brand and our template, and you approve it before anything runs. You never have to send us a JPEG.
  • You see the numbers. Every month you get a short report. Which editions you ran in, how many people opened them, how many clicked you, which one did best, and one idea for next time.
  • It's not only the newsletter. Sponsorships can include our Instagram, the Btown city guide, and the events we run around town.
  • Your category can be yours alone. One dentist. One brokerage. One credit union. When a category is taken, it's taken, and we will tell you honestly whether yours still is.

What we don't run

National brands. Anything we wouldn't send a friend to. Ads that look like ads from somewhere else. If we say no to you, we'll say why.
